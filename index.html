Why · The problem we solve

Every semester, the same story on campus.

A problem hiding in plain sight.

🗑️
4×
Waste spike
Campus waste spikes to 4× the normal volume when exchange students move out — most of it is reusable.
📦
2 weeks
Rushed packing
Two weeks before departure, students start packing. Good items get tossed simply because there is no time to find them a new home.
💸
₩300K+
Startup cost
New arrivals spend over ₩300,000 on bedding, kitchenware, and daily essentials — often buying the exact things that were thrown away weeks earlier.
What's discarded at the end of one semester
is exactly what's needed at the start of the next.
Onloop bridges that gap.

Hand Over

Before leaving, just hand your items to us. We are locating nearby!
